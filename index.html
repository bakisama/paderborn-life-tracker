How to use

  1. Set up. Pick your programme length and first day, then Build my calendar.
  2. Read the grid. Each square is one week, Monday to Sunday. Lectures Break / exams This week Score 1 → 5
  3. Score each week. When a week is over, click or tap its square and rate it 1–5. Add a short note if you like. Keyboard: Tab to a week, Enter, then 1–5.
  4. Catch up. Missed a few weeks? Catch up walks you through them, newest first.
  5. Look back. Hover over a week (or tap it on a phone) to see its dates and your note.
  6. Back up. Your data lives only in this browser. Export a backup now and then; Import restores it on any device.

Dates follow the official NRW / Uni Paderborn calendar. (est.) means not published yet; adjust any date under ⚙ Adjust individual semester dates.

On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Safari can delete a site's data if you don't open it for 7 days; the Home Screen app is exempt but keeps separate data, so move it over with Export / Import.
